## Wording to keep

- Say "real businesses", "storefronts you can walk into", "free business page", "verified figures". These are what exists.
- Do not say it is "live for everyone", "launching", "earn", "invest", "crypto" or "game currency that pays out" (the guide's list of prohibited imagery, and nothing is public yet), and do not quote prices until the policies at `/legal` are final.
- Do not put a tagline between the name and the endorsement, and do not shorten the endorsement ("A Theoretical Minds Technologies Game" is the whole line).
