# ambientCG provenance: the baked ground's grass and dirt

**Files:** `apps/web/public/world/ground/grass.jpg` and `dirt.jpg` (512 x 512, JPEG), the detail the baked ground is textured with (`lib/world/groundTexture.js`).

**Source:** the Color maps of **Grass005** (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass005) and **Ground048** (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground048), the 1K-JPG downloads (`https://ambientcg.com/get?file=<Id>_1K-JPG.zip`, 2026-11-15), re-sized to 512 x 512 and re-saved at JPEG quality 82. **Licence:** **CC0** ("All assets are released under the Creative Commons CC0 license, making them free to use without attribution - even in commercial circumstances", ambientCG's asset pages). The CC0 text is [`../texts/CC0-1.0.txt`](../texts/CC0-1.0.txt). Credit is not required and is given anyway (CREDITS.md).

The other maps of those downloads (normal, roughness, displacement) are not used or shipped. **Recorded on 2026-11-16:** [`Grass005.html`](Grass005.html) and [`Ground048.html`](Ground048.html) are the two asset pages (their published dates are 2025-02-02 and 2021-06-27 and both carry the CC0 line quoted above), and [`license.html`](license.html) is ambientCG's licence page (https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/), all saved as HTML. The pages do not name an author we could credit by name, so the credit is to ambientCG.
