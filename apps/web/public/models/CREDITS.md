# Model credits

The buildings in `kenney/` are from the **Kenney City Kit (Commercial)** and **City Kit (Suburban)**, made by Kenney (https://www.kenney.nl) and released under CC0 (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Crediting Kenney is not required but is appreciated.

The bridge deck and pillars in `kenney/roads/` (the causeways between the landmasses) are from the **Kenney City Kit (Roads)** 2.2, also CC0.

The start gantry, barriers, grandstands, pits, tent, lamp post, trees and race cars in `kenney/racing/` (the racing track) are from the **Kenney Racing Kit** 2.0, also CC0.

The trees and bushes in `kenney/nature/` are from the **Kenney Nature Kit**, and the cars in `kenney/cars/` from the **Kenney Car Kit**, also CC0.

The boats and buoys in `kenney/watercraft/` are from the **Kenney Watercraft Kit**, the docks and platforms in `kenney/pirate/` from the **Kenney Pirate Kit**, and the amusement-park pieces in `kenney/coaster/` (entrance, stalls, coaster train, supports, paths, benches) from the **Kenney Coaster Kit**, all CC0.

The 61 food and drink models in `kenney/food/` and the 6 containers, tanks and solar panels in `kenney/industrial/` (the interior furniture menu: `lib/content/kitItems.js`) are from the **Kenney Food Kit** 2.0 and **City Kit (Industrial)** 2.0, also CC0.

The palms, flowers, fences, logs, crops, campfire and tent among the trees in `kenney/nature/`, and the containers, tank, chimney and solar panels in `kenney/industrial/`, are the yards behind owners' buildings (`lib/content/yardModels.js`).

The containers, tanks, boats, buoys, boat ramp, barrels, crates and palms at the ports and harbours (`lib/world/ports.js`: the front rows of the container yards, the moorings, the channel buoys) are from the **Kenney City Kit (Industrial)**, **Watercraft Kit**, **Pirate Kit** and **Nature Kit** named above, also CC0; a phone draws none of them (docs/world/ports_models.md).

The train and its rail in `kenney/train/` (the train on the first causeway: `lib/world/train.js`) are from the **Kenney Train Kit** 1.1, also CC0.

Voxel landmarks in `vox/` are made for this project (see scripts/models/make-sample-vox.mjs, or your own MagicaVoxel `.vox` files).
